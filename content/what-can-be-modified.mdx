# What can be modified

The game area by area: what you can change today, an example of each, and the tool to use. The full matrix with
every call is on [API status](/api).

<Legend />

## Planets and the planet map

- **Add a planet** to the game's galaxy, with a route from a stock planet. *Example:* a new world above the first planet.
  <Status s="built" /> Studio's Planet window.
- **A galaxy of your own**: an empty map for your planets, switched with L and R. *Example:* the Zephyra Expanse.
  <Status s="built" />
- **Hidden planets** that appear when another is completed, or once you first find their level.
  <Status s="built" /> [Tutorial](/tutorials/planet)

## Levels

- **New levels** from your own Blender geometry: floors, walls, water, lava, pits, invisible colliders.
  <Status s="built" /> [Your first planet](/studio/first-mod)
- **Change a stock level**: add objects to it, change its fog, sky or music, without replacing it. *Example:* a
  portal in the game's first level to your planet. <Status s="built" /> [Tutorial](/tutorials/stock-level)
- **Level look**: sky, clear colour, fog, field of view, weather, light slots, ambient loops. <Status s="built" />
  Studio's Level window.
- **Exits and start points** between your levels and the game's. <Status s="built" />
- Moving platforms, jetpack pads, flying drones' flight paths <Status s="planned" />

## Enemies, friends and objects

- **Place any object the game has**: ant squads, armoured gunners, doors, keys, chests, crates, health, ammo, lights, glows,
  sounds, fog and camera zones, switches. <Status s="built" /> Studio's Prefabs.
- **Squads that lock doors** until they're dead, with navigation regions and patrol networks. <Status s="built" />
- **New enemy looks**: a stock enemy with your textures and size, as its own type. *Example:* the Fire Ant.
  <Status s="built" /> [Tutorial](/tutorials/reskin)
- **Spawning** objects from a trigger or script. <Status s="built" />
- New meshes for enemies <Status s="partial" /> (the data route works; there's no importer yet); enemies with their
  own behaviour <Status s="planned" />

## Weapons

- **Tune the 15 weapons**: damage, fire rate, speed, range, spread, throw timing. *Example:* a pistol for thin air
  that fires faster. <Status s="built" /> [Tutorial](/tutorials/weapons)
- Bolt size, charge speed, mine limit: player-only for now <Status s="partial" />
- **New weapons** with their own gun models and HUD icons <Status s="planned" />

## Story, triggers and scripts

- **Triggers without code**: when the level starts, an area is entered, a door opens, a flag is set, or the hero
  talks to someone, then show messages, play music, fade fog, set flags, unlock doors, warp, spawn, or open and
  complete planets. <Status s="built" />
- **Talkers and notes**: lines that advance each time you talk. <Status s="built" />
- **Lua scripts** for anything the triggers can't say. <Status s="built" /> [Tutorial](/tutorials/script)

## Music and sound

- **Your own music** per level, as OGG with loop points. <Status s="built" /> [Tutorial](/tutorials/music)
- **Any stock tune and ambient loop** in your levels. <Status s="built" />
- Replace or mute sound effects (C) <Status s="built" />; new sound effects <Status s="planned" />

## Look and feel

- **HD texture packs**. <Status s="built" /> [Tutorial](/tutorials/texture-pack)
- **Blood, gore and the hit flash** (C events). <Status s="built" />
- **Hide objects** as they draw (C). <Status s="built" />
- **Translations** of menus, subtitles and hints. <Status s="built" />

## Anything else

A [C code mod](/api/c) can hook or replace any game function. That's the route for changes nothing above covers,
and for [library mods](/api/c#library-mods) that other mods build on.
