# The example world: Zephyra

Zephyra is Studio's example project and the best template to learn from. It's a galaxy of its own, the **Zephyra
Expanse**, with three planets, five new levels and an edit of a stock level. It uses nearly every feature: squads that
lock doors, a key door, a reskinned enemy, talkers, a finale, custom music, weapon tuning and planets that appear one
after another. All of its geometry, textures and music are drawn and synthesized by a script, so it contains no game
data.

## Open it

The project is in Studio's repo at `samples/zephyra.jfgproj`. Open it with **File › Open project…**, or:

```powershell
jfgstudio gui samples\zephyra.jfgproj
```

To play it straight away (from the Studio folder):

```powershell
studio.bat test samples\zephyra.jfgproj --level forest_portal
```

That starts the game in Goldwood's first level with only this pack loaded; the portal is just ahead. Use
`--level landing`, `caverns` or `arena` to start in the world's own levels, and `--start-point 1` for the far end of
a level.

<Callout type="info">
To make your own pack from it, copy the whole folder, change `id` and `name` in `project.json` (your own mod id), and
start changing levels. The generator script `samples/make_example_world.py` rewrites the sample from scratch, so edit
your copy, not the sample.
</Callout>

## What's in the project

| Folder | In Zephyra |
|---|---|
| `geometry/` | `landing.glb`, `caverns.glb`, `arena.glb`, `glacia.glb`, `nimbus.glb`: the five levels' models |
| `levels/` | Six levels: the five above, and `forest_portal`, an edit of Goldwood's stock Forest First |
| `kinds/` | `fire_ant`: a BlueAnt with fiery textures |
| `galaxies/` | `expanse`: the Zephyra Expanse, home planet Zephyra |
| `planets/` | `zephyra`, `glacia`, `nimbus` |
| `music/` | Three OGG tracks |
| `triggers.json` | Twelve triggers: intros, a talker, a finale, the planet completions |
| `weapons.json` | Weapon 0 (the starting gun) fires faster with faster bolts |
| `scripts/` | `zephyra.lua`, a hand-written script that logs how long you take |

## The route

<Steps>
### Forest First (stock, edited)

The pack adds an exit to Goldwood's first level without replacing it: a **portal** with a blue glow, a light just
ahead of the new-game start point. In Studio it's the level `forest_portal`, made with **Edit a stock level…** (its
own objects show in a dim blue and can't be selected). See [Change a stock level](/tutorials/stock-level).

- East: a ramp up to a plateau with a **chest** (the machine gun) and a **scout's note**: a talker that tells its story

### Ember Caverns

<Figure src="/img/example-caverns.png" alt="Ember Caverns: an orange lava lake with a stone causeway and plaza" caption="Ember Caverns: a lava lake (it burns), a causeway and a plaza." />

- A **Fire Ant squad** on the plaza: the pack's own enemy kind (below), and a ZombieAnt squad on the far shore.
- A chasm with a deadly drop (a `kill` floor) and a narrow bridge.
- The north door needs the yellow key. Crates for cover, orange lights, the lava slowly flowing (a texture scroll).

### Sky Arena

- A floating arena over a deadly drop. A stone screen hides the entry, with cover walls and four pillars.
- A **BigShieldAnt squad** and a **Cyborg squad** both lock the final gate. When it opens, a `door opens` trigger
  shows a message, changes the music, and **spawns** full health next to you.
- Behind the gate, the **victory exit** completes Zephyra and leads on to Glacia.

## How the pieces are built

### The galaxy and its hidden planets

The three planets are on the pack's own galaxy, the **Zephyra Expanse**. On the planet map the player switches to it
with R and back with L.

| Planet | Opens | Entry level |
|---|---|---|
| Zephyra (home) | **discovered**: once its level was entered (through the portal) | Zephyra Landing |
| Glacia | once Zephyra was **completed** | Glacia |
| Nimbus | once Glacia was **completed** | Nimbus |

Each planet's levels are its region (the levels' **Planet**), so the Sky Arena's victory exit, Glacia's pad and
Nimbus's way home show the game's stats screen before the next planet, and each planet greets the hero with its name.
Zephyra's three levels share one region: moving between them shows nothing.

A world counts as completed when a trigger's `complete planet` action runs: Zephyra's is the `victory` trigger at the
arena's exit, Glacia's the pad on the rise. See [A galaxy of your own](/tutorials/planet).

<Figure src="/img/studio-level-graph.png" alt="The Level Graph: each level with its exits and where they lead, then the planets' entry levels" caption="The Level Graph lists every exit and where it leads, then each planet's entry level." />

### The Fire Ant

<Figure src="/img/studio-kinds.png" alt="The Kinds window for Fire Ant: base BlueAnt, size 1.00, ten replaced texture slots" caption="The Fire Ant kind: the BlueAnt's model and behaviour with ten new textures." width={345} />

A **kind** is a stock enemy with your textures at your size. The pack carries only the ten PNGs; the port builds the
model and definition from each player's own game. The caverns' squadron spawns Fire Ants because its `kindFields`
name the kind. See [A new enemy look](/tutorials/reskin).

### Triggers and the finale

<Figure src="/img/studio-triggers.png" alt="The Triggers window: the portal trigger and the landing intro" caption="Each level greets you with a message; the scout's note, the fire ants' warning and the finale are triggers too." width={345} />

| Trigger | When | Then |
|---|---|---|
| the portal | level start, Forest First | message |
| landing intro (and one per level) | level start | message |
| scout's note | talk, by the chest, every time | lines (one per visit) |
| fire ants | enter an area in the caverns | message |
| both squads down | door opens (the arena's gate) | message, music, spawn full health by the hero |
| victory | enter the exit area | message, complete planet Zephyra, fade the fog |
| glacia complete, nimbus complete | enter the finish areas | complete planet |

### A script of its own

`scripts/zephyra.lua` sits beside the triggers: it logs each level and how long you take to reach the arena's finale.
Open it in the **Scripts** window.
