# Squads that move

A squad is a **squadron** (the entry that runs the group), its **members** (the enemies) and a **navigation
region**, the squad area they keep to. Getting the members to spawn and fire is easy. Getting them to walk, turn round
and come after the player takes a few more things, and each rule on this page was found in the decomp and
play-tested in a new level.

Studio sets all of it up when it makes a squad, and the validator names whatever is missing.

The pictures are Studio's top-down preview of a test yard: the squad area in red, the squadron as a red square and
its members as red dots, holes in pink, a patrol route in blue. Up is −z.

## The squad area

A squad area is a polygon of region corners, numbered in order round it. Seen from above, with x to the right and
z down the page, the corners must go **counter-clockwise**. The game finds nothing inside a clockwise area, so its
squad stays where it spawned and never turns.

Studio winds every area counter-clockwise, whichever way you click the corners. The validator stops a clockwise one
(`region-winding`), and `fix_squad` turns it round.

Members only notice the player while the player is inside an area too (any region numbered 1 or more). Make the area
cover the floor the squad fights on and the way the player comes in.

## Cover

Smart squads (`behaviour` 0x11, used by many of the game's ant squads) only move to **cover**: corners the player
can't see round. Those are the corners of **holes**, obstacles cut out of the area, and the area's own inward
corners.

<Figure src="/img/squads-no-cover.png" alt="A rectangular squad area with two crates inside it that are not holes" caption="No cover: the crates are only geometry. The ants crouch and fire and hardly ever move." />

In a plain rectangle there is no such corner, so the members crouch and fire. Almost every squad in the game has an
area shaped round its room, with holes for its pillars and crates.

<Figure src="/img/squads-cover.png" alt="The same squad area with each crate cut out as a pink crossed hole" caption="With cover: each crate is a hole. The ants run between the crates, turn and come after the player, and keep out of the crates." />

A hole is a region of its own, wound **clockwise** round the obstacle, with a **region settings** entry (flags 1)
that links it to the squad area. Members run to its corners and keep out of it, so they don't walk into the obstacle.

### Setting up cover

1. Put something to hide behind inside the squad area: blocks, crates, pillars or low walls, at least 40 units
   taller than the floor. Keep them off the edge of the area. For an obstacle on the edge, draw the area's outline
   round it instead, since an inward corner is cover too.
2. Make the obstacles holes:
   - **Editor**: a new squad (*Groups › Squad, region and locked door*) gets holes round the obstacles in its area by
     itself. For a squad made before, or after you add obstacles, select the squadron and press **Add cover** in the
     Inspector. To draw a hole by hand, pick the **Region** tool (<kbd>G</kbd>), tick **Hole** in the toolbar and
     click the corners round the obstacle.
   - **Agent tools**: `place_squad` adds cover itself; `add_cover(level, squadron)` does it for an existing squad.
3. Run the validator. It warns about a smart squad whose area is a plain shape with no holes (`squad-cover`).

## Patrols

A patrol squad walks a route of **patrol points** and fights like a smart squad once it sees the player: it turns,
takes cover and comes after them. Walking the route is play-tested; the switch to fighting follows the decomp.

<Figure src="/img/squads-patrol.png" alt="The squad area with its two holes and a blue ring of six patrol points round the middle" caption="A patrol: a ring of six points. The squad starts from the point nearest to it and walks the ring until it spots the player." />

How a squad walks its route:

- It starts from the patrol point nearest the squadron, so place the squadron next to the route.
- At each point it picks a link it didn't come along. A chain is walked there and back; a ring goes round and round.
- It walks straight from point to point with no path-finding, so keep every leg of the route clear of obstacles and
  wide enough for the whole squad.
- Never give a patrol point a **start value** (`startAtLoad`). It means "ride this point's lift", and the squad
  nearest it stops at its first point for good. The validator warns (`patrol-start`).

### Setting up a patrol

- **Agent tools**: `place_patrol(level, points, loop, squadron)` lays the points along `points`, links them in order
  (`loop` joins the last to the first) and makes the squadron a patrol. It copies a stock patrol squad of the same
  enemy and sets the behaviours. A squad more than 300 from its route is moved to the route's first point, with its
  members and area.
- **Editor**:
  1. Pick the **Patrol** tool (<kbd>P</kbd>) and click the floor for each point. Each one links back to the one
     before. Click an existing point to link to it, which closes a ring.
  2. Select the squadron and set, in the Inspector: `kind` 6 (patrol), `behaviour` 2 (walk the route),
     `alertBehaviour` and `hurtBehaviour` 17 (smart: fight once the player is seen or shoots).
  3. Move the squadron next to the first point, and keep the route inside its area.

<Callout type="info">
To watch a patrol without being seen, turn on **Tools › Cheats › Enemies ignore you** in the game (F10) before the
squad spots you. With it on, squads don't see you, hear anything or react when shot. Patrols keep walking, and smart
squads stand where they are.
</Callout>

## Spawning

A copied stock squadron spawns when a hero walks into a zone placed for **its own level**, so in a new level it may
never appear. Studio's squads spawn as soon as the level starts (`trigger` 3). The validator warns about one that
still waits for a stock zone (`squad-trigger`).

## The squadron entry

Some of a squadron's bytes aren't decoded yet, so Studio copies them from a stock squad of the **same enemy** and the
**same mode**. It picks a smart one (`behaviour` 0x11) before any other, and never a sentry (9), which never walks and
only faces one way. Any member type works, the pack's own enemy kinds included. `fix_squad` mends a squad that copies
the wrong one (`squad-stock`), makes it spawn at once and turns a clockwise area round.

## Validator rules

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| `region-winding` | The squad area goes clockwise: its squad stands still. Error. |
| `squad-cover` | A smart squad's area has no holes and no inward corner: the members stand and fire. |
| `squad-trigger` | The squad waits for a stock level's zone and may never spawn. |
| `squad-stock` | The squadron copies another enemy's squad, the wrong mode or a sentry. |
| `patrol-start` | A patrol point has a start value: the squad near it stops at its first point. |
| `squad-region` | The squadron's area has no corners in this level: the game crashes when the level starts. Error. |
