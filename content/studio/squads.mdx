# Squads that move

A squad is a **squadron** (the entry that runs the group), its **members** (the enemies) and a **navigation
region** they keep to. Getting the members to spawn and fire is easy. Getting them to walk, turn round and come after
the player needs several things to be right. Each rule below was found in the decomp and play-tested in a new level.

Studio sets all of it up when it makes a squad, and the validator names whatever is missing.

## The rules

### The region goes counter-clockwise

A region is a polygon of region corners, numbered in order around it. Seen from above with x to the right and z
down the page, the corners must go **counter-clockwise**. The game finds nothing inside a clockwise region, so its
squad stays where it spawned and never turns. The game's own clockwise regions are holes (below), not areas.

Studio winds every region it makes counter-clockwise, whichever way you click the corners. The validator stops a
clockwise one (`region-winding`); `fix_squad` turns it round.

### There must be cover

Smart squads (`behaviour` 0x11, used by many of the game's ant squads) only move to **cover**: corners the player can't
see round. Those are the corners of **holes** (obstacles cut out of the region) and the region's own inward corners.

In a plain rectangle over open floor there is no such corner, so the members crouch and fire and hardly ever move.
Almost every squad in the game has a region shaped round its room, with holes for pillars and crates.

So give a squad things to hide behind inside its region: blocks, crates, pillars, low walls. Each one is made a
hole: a region of its own, wound **clockwise** round the obstacle, with a **region settings** entry (flags 1)
linking it to the squad's region. The members run to its corners, and keep out of it, so they don't walk into the
obstacle.

- `place_squad` and `add_cover` (the agent tools) find the obstacles in the region and make them holes.
- An obstacle that crosses the region's edge isn't made a hole: draw the region's outline round it instead (an
  inward corner is cover too).
- The validator warns about a smart squad whose region is a plain shape with no holes (`squad-cover`).

### The player must be in a region

Members only notice the player when the player stands inside a region too (any region numbered 1 or more). Make a squad's
region cover the floor they fight on and the way the player comes in.

### Spawning

A copied stock squadron spawns when a hero walks into a zone placed for **its own level**, so in a new level it may
never appear. Studio's squads spawn as soon as the level starts (`trigger` 3). The validator warns about one that
still waits for a stock zone (`squad-trigger`).

### The squadron entry

Some of a squadron's bytes aren't decoded yet, so Studio copies them from a stock squad of the **same enemy** and the
**same mode**. It picks a smart one (`behaviour` 0x11) before any other, and never a sentry (9), which never walks and
only faces one way. Any member type works, the pack's own enemy kinds included. `fix_squad` mends a squad that copies
the wrong one (`squad-stock`).

## Patrols

A patrol squad walks a route of patrol points (`place_patrol`) and fights like a smart squad once it sees the player.

- It walks straight from point to point with no path-finding, so keep each leg of the route clear of obstacles and
  wide enough for the whole squad.
- Never give a patrol point a **start value**. It means "ride this point's lift": the squad nearest it stops at its
  first point for good. The validator warns (`patrol-start`).

<Callout type="info">
Patrols are taken from the decomp and haven't been play-tested yet.
</Callout>

## Validator rules

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| `region-winding` | The region goes clockwise: its squad stands still. Error. |
| `squad-cover` | A smart squad's region has no holes and no inward corner: the members stand and fire. |
| `squad-trigger` | The squad waits for a stock level's zone and may never spawn. |
| `squad-stock` | The squadron copies another enemy's squad, the wrong mode or a sentry. |
| `patrol-start` | A patrol point has a start value: the squad near it stops at its first point. |
| `squad-region` | The squadron's region has no corners in this level: the game crashes when the level starts. Error. |
