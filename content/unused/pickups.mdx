# Pickups and weapon 15

## Weapon 15, a cut proximity mine

The weapon table has a complete sixteenth row (weapon 15: speed 24, ammo 100 / 20, start 100), but nothing gives it.
Its pickup (0x126) is never placed, and no squadron drops it.

There is more of it: a front-end icon (0x247) and a second proximity mine description in the menu text (strings
254-258). The timed mines' hint mentions a "proximity mode", so it may have been folded into the timed mines.

**What it does as shipped** (checked in the decomp):
- Given to the hero, weapon 15 fires the default laser bolt, not mines: the code that fires weapons has no case
  for it.
- The HUD names it with text 72, "Pistol Ammo", because there are only 15 weapon names.
- Its pickup works as a plain 12-byte list entry.

So the mine was planned (table row, icon, text) but its firing code was never written. **Seen in the port:** fired
through the timed mines' code, with a copy of the remote mines' row and its name pointed at "Proximity Mine", the
hero throws up to 10 mines and they go off.

## Five key colours nothing uses

The game has ten key colours. Yellow, red and magenta keys are placed in levels; green and blue come from chests
(see [Turned out to be used](/unused#turned-out-to-be-used)). The other five, **light blue, lime, purple, tango and
lemon**, are unused: no list places their pickups, no chest gives them, no door asks for them and no code hands them
out. Menu text exists for all of them ("... Key Required" and the key names).

Placed by a pack, their pickups and doors work. A door needs a key when its entry +0x12 is the key's item + 2 (light
blue 6, lime 7, purple 8, tango 9, lemon 10). Seen in the port: the lime key and a lime key door with its lock
lights.

Two more have no model of their own:
- **Invincibility (0x33C)** works in single player: invulnerability plus the shield.
- **Invisibility (0x12A)** only half works there: the hero is still drawn, but enemies stop hearing and noticing him.

Both are named only by the multiplayer pickup setup. There is also a pyramid-shaped pickup model with no object: see
[Models](/unused/models).
