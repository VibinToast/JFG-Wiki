# Other unused animations

127 of the game's 1,174 animations (about 70 KB) are in no model's list. Each is played whole and looping, at 24 frames a second, on a model with as many joints as the animation has channels: the model its animation is stored next to, or for the two big blocks the heroes' menu and cutscene bodies. That model is a best guess, so a pose can look wrong where the animation was made for another rig. Click a still to play it.

The three pages: [510-575](/unused/animations-menus-1), [828-877](/unused/animations-menus-2), [the rest](/unused/animations-other).
