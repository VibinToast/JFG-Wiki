# Unused sprites

22 of the game's 107 sprites have no use found: 39-41, 44-46, 49, 66, 70, 75, 76, 78-83, 90, 95, 96, 98 and 103. The explosion table wasn't decoded, so some may be used there. Every frame is shown left to right, scaled up; the grey shows through the transparent pixels. The water boss's sprite (36) is on [Enemies](/unused/enemies).
