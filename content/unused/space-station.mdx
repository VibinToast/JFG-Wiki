# The space station ends at a dead end

The space station (world 12) has four playable rooms in a line, all on one tune, built from a developer's sewer demo
rooms:

```
map -> 275 landing (cutscene) -> 276 <-> 104 <-> 264 <-> 9
```

Level 276 also exits to the take-off cutscene (331), then 271 and the ending sequence (93).

- **Level 9's only exit leads back to 264** (checked by hand: level 9 uses track 8 and lists 9 and 412).
- Level 9 has one locked door at (653, 7, 328), door id 1. It opens when its squadron (0x1E, five blue ants) is
  dead. The other twelve doors of the same kind in 104 and 264 are unlocked.
- Behind it, segment 15 of track 8 is a corridor (x 418 to 822, z 283 to 384) that ends in a closed wall. There is
  no exit, start point or level end there.

So the last fight opens a door onto a stub: the joint for a room that was never built. The Japanese version also
removes three squadrons and three blue ants from level 104.

## To restore it

1. Geometry joined to the end of segment 15: a new track (id 301 is free) or an edit of track 8.
2. A level for it. Reuse an unused level id such as 83 (both its object lists are empty), in world 12, region 26,
   region index 4, on the space station's tune (55).
3. An exit behind the door in level 9 into the new level, and a new start point in 9 for the way back.
4. An ending, for example an exit to 331, the take-off cutscene.

JFG-Studio can make the new level and its exits: see [Make a planet](/tutorials/planet) and
[Edit a stock level](/tutorials/stock-level).
