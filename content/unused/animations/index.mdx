# Animations

127 of the game's 1,174 animations (about 70 KB) are in no model's list. The two big blocks, 510-575 and 828-877,
are the heroes' menu and cutscene animations.

Each is played whole and looping, at 24 frames a second, on a model with as many joints as the animation has
channels: the model it is stored next to, or for the two big blocks the heroes' menu and cutscene bodies. That model
is a best guess, so a pose can look wrong where the animation was made for another rig. Each page shows stills until
you click one, so it loads only what you scroll to.
