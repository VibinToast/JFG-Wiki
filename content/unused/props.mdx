Also never placed, with no picture here:
- **Hint characters 0x1E7 and 0x1E9.** They wander and talk like the stock hint characters, and can't be killed.
  0x1E7 has no hint text of its own (script 8 is a blank placeholder, perhaps its slot).
- **Vehicles:** an ant transporter (0x5D) and a lift car (0x49). Seen: the lift car rides its path.
- **Door switches 5, 7, 8, 9 and 10** (0xD4, 0xD6-0xD9). Seen: they work as lock lights beside a key door.
- **The light show (0x2C2):** rotating coloured lamps with fading ribbon trails. It works as it is.
- **A spinning light (0x62):** a turning spot light with a lighthouse-style glare. A period of 0 hangs the game.
- **A bouncy bridge (0xBC):** the stock long wooden bridge with another model. It bounces only when something lands
  on it falling, not when walked onto.
- **A path junction (0x7),** with its own code, and **an audio line (0x19F).**
- **Three doors** (0x46, 0x48, 0x5A) only in two object lists that no level uses.

## Code with no object

These behaviours have code but no object definition uses them. Each needs a new definition with that behaviour (a
pack object type) and a placement.

- **Frog (behaviour 21):** sits, then hops 40-72 units, hops away from a hero who comes close, keeps within a range
  of its start, and is squashed flat or vanishes when touched. Its code expects a 64-byte data block the game never
  gives it, which crashes. Seen in the port with that fixed: frogs load. No frog model survives.
- **Buoy (16):** a bobbing solid prop. No buoy model survives.
- **Timed target (119):** a multiplayer target that can't be damaged and leaves after a time, a sibling of the stock
  pop-up target. Seen: it shows, then leaves on time.
- **Sky switch (10):** changes the sky when a hero comes within its radius.
- **No-gravity box (31).**
- **Flare switch (13)** only sets its scale, and **wave swell (14)** does nothing.
