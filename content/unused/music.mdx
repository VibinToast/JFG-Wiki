# Music

The game has 90 music sequences.

- **Full songs nothing plays:** 59 (201 seconds, 3,335 notes), 63 (108 seconds) and 89 (82 seconds).
- **Shorter unused pieces:** 44, 25, 22, 24, 3 (ambient-like) and 26.
- **Empty placeholders** (97 bytes, no notes): 6, 8, 9, 10, 28, 34, 36, 40 and 45.
- **Used by only one level:** 21, 23, 33, 39, 54 and 67. In effect unused if that level can't be reached.

To hear one in the game, a pack can set it as a level's tune: see [Add music](/tutorials/music).

Sound effects weren't surveyed: 112 of the 436 places that play one pass a computed id, and many sounds come from
data tables not decoded yet.
