# Enemies

All of these are squad enemies: a squadron entry names their type, and the squad code (overlay 3) runs them. What
each does comes from the decomp; "seen" means tried in the port's test hub.

How the shared parts work:
- **Attack.** A type with an attack animation fires whatever that animation's fire set says. A type with **no**
  attack animation turns to face the hero and fires one laser bolt instead. Several cut types have their own bolt
  sound, so they were meant to shoot at some point.
- **Death** depends on the type: removed at once; **flung** (thrown up spinning, then a blast where it lands); a
  **death animation, then a fade** (a type with no dying animation keeps its body); or **electrocuted**, then an
  explosion.
- **Flyers fly at the squadron's height.** A flying squadron placed on the floor hovers at the floor. The developers'
  test rooms put flyers 84 units above it.

## The water boss

A creature of glowing orbs whose tentacles sway, then reach for the hero when he comes close. Object 0xE6 is an
ordinary squad enemy drawn with sprite 36, and overlay 152 holds only its code. Nothing names it: no level,
squadron, text or cutscene, so which water level it was for is unknown.

- **Body:** always 5 tentacles of 8 orbs, depth-sorted. The 40 orbs are also its 40 hit spheres. The orbs pulse in
  size along each tentacle.
- **Scale:** the squadron's member scale byte, divided by 142 rather than the usual 100, sizes the tentacles, their
  reach, the trigger range and the orbs.
- **Waving:** each tentacle sways in slow circles, with a new heading eased in every so often and a ripple running
  down the chain.
- **Reaching:** when the hero comes within 1500 × scale, the tentacles blend into a reach towards him, at most
  720 × scale long. Past 2000 × scale they blend back into waving.
- **Hits:** a shot tentacle flashes red for one frame.
- **Immune to some things:** it can't be electrocuted and the aim won't lock on to it.
- **One per level:** its state is one shared block, not per object. Every member of its squadron redraws all 40 orbs,
  so a squadron of more than one stacks them (and slows the game).
- **No attack of its own:** reaching only moves the orbs.

**It can't die in the shipped code.** It has normal health, but every hit is scaled by the struck sphere's damage
percent, and its 40 orb spheres get 0. Every weapon deals nothing: shots spark on it and it never dies. It may have
been scenery to get past, or unfinished. Seen in the port: with the orb spheres at 100 percent, it dies after its
health's worth of hits.

To place one: a squadron of count 1 with enemy type 0xE6, sentry behaviour (9) in all three behaviour bytes, member
scale 142, about 1500 units of open floor, facing the way the hero comes in, and only one per level.
