# Change a stock level

Add a portal to the game's first level that leads to your planet, without replacing the level. Your pack carries
only the change, so it works with every player's copy and alongside other packs that change the same level.

<Steps>
### Start an edit of the level

In the **Project** window press **Edit a stock level…**, search for the level (the game's first level is level 21), and
**Create**. Studio makes a level folder with `base: stock:level/21`.

### Find your way around

The level's own objects show in a dim blue in the Scene: you can see them, not select or change them. Fly to the
spot you want; the new-game start point is a good landmark.

### Add the portal

From **Prefabs**, drag in an exit (**Players & exits**) and a glow (**Lights & effects**) so players can see it. Set
the exit's **Leads to** to your level and its start point.

### Optionally change the level's look

The **Level** window's fields write over the stock header: fog, sky, music. Leave them alone to keep the level as it
was.

### Play

**Play** starts the game in that level with your pack loaded; walk to the portal.
</Steps>

## What happens in the pack

Studio writes your change as a **patch** of the level's object list, not a replacement: an append of your objects.
Another pack can patch the same level and both apply. A patch that expects an object the player's data doesn't have
is skipped and logged instead of breaking the level.

<Callout type="info">
The example world's `forest_portal` level is exactly this: a portal, a glow and a light added to the game's first level.
</Callout>
