# A galaxy of your own

Give your planets their own planet map, so they never crowd the game's galaxy, and make each one appear only once the
one before it is beaten, as the game's own planets do. This is how the example world's Zephyra Expanse works.

<Figure src="/img/studio-planet.webp" alt="The Planet window showing the Zephyra Expanse with Zephyra, Glacia and Nimbus" caption="A galaxy of the pack's own: an empty grid with only its planets and routes." />

<Steps>
### Make the first planet and its galaxy

In **Planet**, press **New planet**. Under **Galaxy**, choose **New galaxy…**. Give the galaxy a **Galaxy name** (it's
shown at the top of the map) and leave this planet as its **home**: the cursor starts there.

### Place it and choose when it opens

Click the empty grid to place the disc. Set **Entry level** for each hero. For **Opens**:

| Option | The planet shows… |
|---|---|
| from the start | as soon as the pack is loaded |
| once its own level was entered | after the player first reaches its level some other way, e.g. a portal (it's "discovered") |
| by a trigger or script | when a trigger's `unlock planet` action or `jfg.unlock_planet` runs |
| once another planet was entered | after the player has been to another planet |
| once another planet was completed | after another planet is completed |

The home planet should open from the start (or be discovered), or the galaxy never shows.

### Add the next planet

**New planet** again (it joins the galaxy you're looking at). Click the first planet to draw the route from it: only
free stick directions are offered. Set **Opens** to *once another planet was completed* and **After planet** to the
first.

### Complete the first world

A planet is completed by a trigger. At the end of the first world's last level, make a trigger: **When** `enter`
with the finish area (select objects there and press **Area from the Scene's selection**), and the action
`complete planet` with the first planet.

### Play it

Save and **Play**. Reach the finish: the next planet appears on the map. On the planet map, **R** switches to your
galaxy and **L** back to the game's (on a controller also RB/LB and the D-pad).
</Steps>

## Planet regions

In the game, each planet is split into **regions**. Leaving a region by an exit shows the **"Area Cleared"** stats
screen (Tribals rescued, killed and remaining, the time, then your weapons), and entering one shows its name with the
planet's and your continues. Your planets work the same way once their levels name them:

<Steps>
### Give each level its planet

In the **Level** window, set **Planet** on every level of the planet. A planet's levels together are one region: moving
between them shows nothing; an exit to another planet's level, or to a stock level, shows the stats screen first.

### Keep the exits' stats flag

Exits have **arg1A** 1 by default, as most of the game's exits do. Set it to 0 on an exit that should leave without
the stats screen.

### Play it

Leave the planet: the stats screen waits for a button press, then the next level loads with the new planet's title:
the planet's name, the galaxy's name under it, and your continues.
</Steps>

Being a region brings the game's other region rules with it:

- **Continues**: two each time the hero enters the planet. Dying with none left is game over.
- **Restarts**: after a death the hero starts again at the level where the planet was entered.
- **Leaving resets the region** you came from: its Tribal progress and killed squads start again when you return,
  as in the game. A portal out of a stock level shows that stock region's stats screen too.
- **Records**: the stats screen keeps the region's best kills and time in the save, as the game's regions do.

Jet Force Gemini: Recompiled gives mods' regions the numbers 31–39 (the game uses 1–30; the save keeps 40), so all the mods loaded
together have room for **nine** planets with regions. A planet can have up to 32 levels. Studio warns past either
limit.

<Callout type="info">
Opened, entered and completed planets are kept per save file, in `saves/<save>_worlds.json` next to the game's save,
written when the game saves.
</Callout>

## From code

```lua
jfg.complete_planet("glacia")            -- open what comes after Glacia
local open, entered, done = jfg.planet("nimbus")
jfg.show_galaxy("expanse")               -- the next time the map opens, it's on the Expanse
```

C mods have `jfg_world_*` and `jfg_galaxy_*`, and the events `jfg_on_map_open`, `jfg_on_galaxy_change` and
`jfg_on_planet_select` ([C API](/api/c)).

## On the game's galaxy instead

Leave **Galaxy** on the game's galaxy, click a stock planet to route from, and pick **with the stock point its route
leaves**: your planet shows once the player has that stock planet. If another pack already uses that direction from
that planet, Jet Force Gemini: Recompiled moves yours to a free one (and logs it).
